Hi, I'm Byte - quick start

  1. Filter with the boxes at the top: category, drug type, risk band, time period, transport, or type a route or place in the search box.
  2. Check Alerts and the tiles to see what stands out right now.
  3. Open the Route map and tap a line or a number to read the reports behind it.
  4. Read the Events table and open the article link to verify. Use How risk is scored to see why an event is High, Medium or Low.
  5. Use My watchlist to follow a vessel, container or term, and What to look for for indicator cards.
Everything here is a lead to verify, not proof. The clip has sound, turn it up if you like.
