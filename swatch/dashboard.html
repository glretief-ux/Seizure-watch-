What to look for (indicator cards)

Practical starting points in the style of PCCP risk indicators. They are general guidance compiled from common customs practice and from the reports in this tool. They are not official PCCP or WCO material, so check them against your training and local procedures. An indicator is a reason to look closer, never proof. Use several together, base checks on cargo, documents and route (not on a person's nationality or appearance), and follow your agency's legal powers and safety rules.
