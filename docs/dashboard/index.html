Definitions and limits

Event. One seizure, with the news and official reports about it merged. Risk score. 0–100, built from five dimensions: concealment and method, insiders and organised crime, route, scale and commodity. Seizure weight is capped at 10 points. Bands. High, Medium and Low as assigned by Seizure Watch.

Filters. Each chart ignores its own selection so you can compare alternatives, and respects every other one. Selecting several items in one chart shows events matching any of them.

Left out. Every event is unverified news-based reporting, so counts follow what is published and which languages are searched, not what is seized. Quantities are only available where a report states a weight, and transport type is unknown for most events, so it is left out of the transport chart. The repeat vessel or container flag is left out of the flags chart. Countries without a map position (Hong Kong, Taiwan, Ivory Coast, Cape Verde) are counted everywhere except on the map.

Source. The events export of this repository (docs/events.csv), read directly by this page, so it follows the daily update.
